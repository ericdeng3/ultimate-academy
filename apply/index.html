Who This Is For

Is this you?

  • You're already playing club or league ultimate, 1-3 times a week
  • You're mid-20s to mid-40s, juggling a full-time job or shift work around training
  • You're not chasing a national team spot — you want a bigger impact on the field, not a pro career

This isn't for you if

  • You're looking for in-person sessions or a fully custom 1:1 program

The Approach

What most training advice gets wrong for ultimate players.

Most programs assume more training days means more results.

This one assumes the opposite — the right 2-3 gym days plus one field day beats five generic days stacked onto a schedule that's already full.

Most strength programs are borrowed from another sport.

This one is built around what ultimate actually demands — cutting, deceleration, first-step acceleration, layout mechanics.

Most training advice treats getting stronger and staying available all season as separate goals.

For an adult player, they're the same goal — the strongest athlete in the world is worth nothing on the sideline.
